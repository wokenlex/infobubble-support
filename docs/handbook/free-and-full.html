Free and full version, privacy

What the free version includes, how to unlock everything, and what leaves your Mac.

Free version

  • Up to 100 sources are fetched. More can be added, but only the first 100 are processed.
  • Up to 3 tabs and 2 workspaces.
  • Everything else, including AI features, the catalog and export, is the same.

Full version

A one-time purchase, no subscription, shared with your Family Sharing group. Choose Buy → Buy full version in the menu bar. On another Mac use Buy → Restore Purchase. After purchase the menu shows License → Lifetime License.

Privacy

There are no InfoBubble servers and no tracking. Your project, news, analysis, translations and Apple Intelligence all stay on your Mac. The network is used only to:

  • download feeds, pages and site icons from the sources you added;
  • talk to an AI service you configured (article text and your criteria are sent to it);
  • send exports where you told them to go;
  • load map tiles and translation packs from Apple.
